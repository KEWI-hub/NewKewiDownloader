Prism browser integration

Settings follow the Prism app.

Hand matching browser downloads to Prism.

A floating button appears when you hover a video.

Hold this key while clicking a link to let the browser download it.

Extensions separated by spaces. Wildcards allowed (e.g. R0*).

One per line. Entries ending in /, - or * are prefixes.

Smaller downloads (when the size is known) stay in the browser. 0 = no limit.

One per line: example.com, *.example.com or https://example.com/path/*.

On these sites the video button asks the app for available formats.
